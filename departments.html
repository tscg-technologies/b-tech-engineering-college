ACADEMIC DEPARTMENTS

ENGINEERING DEPARTMENTS

Dedicated faculties delivering subject specialization, applied research, and state-of-the-art laboratory training.

Home / Departments
DEPARTMENT 01

Department of Computer Science & Engineering

Fostering expertise in computational theory, software development life cycles, and systems programming.

The department integrates foundational computing concepts with modern application development methodologies. Faculty guidance focuses on algorithmic optimization and scalable code execution.

Key Subjects & Core Topics:
Programming Data Structures Algorithms Database Management Software Engineering Web Technologies

Practical Learning: Linux lab coding sessions, object-oriented design patterns, database query tuning, and version-controlled team sprints.

Related Careers: Software Engineer, Systems Architect, Cloud Developer, Database Administrator.

DEPARTMENT 02

Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Focusing on mathematical intelligence, deep representation learning, computer vision, and cognitive systems.

Equipped with GPU computing facilities, the department provides an academic pathway into algorithmic learning from complex multi-dimensional datasets.

Key Subjects & Core Topics:
Artificial Intelligence Machine Learning Deep Learning Neural Networks Intelligent Systems Pattern Recognition

Practical Learning: Python machine learning toolkits, model training on high-performance GPUs, hyperparameter fine-tuning, and open-source benchmark testing.

Related Careers: AI Specialist, ML Engineer, Computer Vision Researcher, Robotics Intelligence Architect.

DEPARTMENT 03

Department of Data Science

Bridging statistics, distributed computational frameworks, predictive analysis, and visual storytelling.

The department emphasizes turning raw informational feeds into actionable mathematical models through rigorous analysis and distributed processing.

Key Subjects & Core Topics:
Statistics Python Programming Data Analytics Data Visualization Big Data Concepts Data Mining

Practical Learning: Large-scale tabular analysis, data extraction, dashboard creation, distributed querying with Hadoop and Spark simulation labs.

Related Careers: Data Analyst, Big Data Engineer, Quantitative Consultant, Business Intelligence Architect.

DEPARTMENT 04

Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering

Pioneering hardware system development, microelectronics, RF communication, and embedded device architecture.

Equipped with circuit laboratories and signal workstations, students master electronic component physics, printed circuit design, and wireless signal transmission.

Key Subjects & Core Topics:
Digital Electronics Communication Systems Embedded Systems VLSI Fundamentals Signal Processing Microcontrollers

Practical Learning: Breadboard prototyping, oscilloscope measurements, FPGA design synthesis, circuit simulation via SPICE, and embedded C programming.

Related Careers: VLSI Design Engineer, Embedded Firmware Developer, Telecommunications Specialist, Hardware Test Engineer.

DEPARTMENT 05

Department of Electrical & Electronics Engineering

Dedicated to power dynamics, generation grids, electromagnetic machinery, and industrial solid-state electronics.

The department trains students in high-power systems, sustainable energy integration, smart grids, and closed-loop electronic controllers.

Key Subjects & Core Topics:
Electrical Machines Power Systems Control Systems Power Electronics Microgrid Management High Voltage Engineering

Practical Learning: Dynamometer testing of electric motors, transformer synchronization tests, MATLAB/Simulink stability analysis, and solar inverter circuits.

Related Careers: Power Grid Engineer, Renewable Energy Systems Consultant, Industrial Automation Specialist, EV Subsystems Engineer.

DEPARTMENT 06

Department of Mechanical Engineering

Centering on manufacturing science, kinematics, thermo-fluid systems, CAD/CAM modelling, and industrial robotics.

Featuring mechanical workshops, fluid flow flumes, and CAD computational suites, students develop a tactile understanding of physical mechanism design and thermal efficiency.

Key Subjects & Core Topics:
Manufacturing Thermodynamics CAD/CAM Machine Design Robotics Fundamentals Heat Transfer

Practical Learning: Turning, shaping, and welding exercises, CNC code generation, stress-strain material tests, and 3D parametric CAD modeling.

Related Careers: Mechanical Design Engineer, Production Specialist, Thermal Systems Analyst, Automation & Robotics Engineer.

DEPARTMENT 07

Department of Civil Engineering

Focusing on structural mechanics, soil dynamics, sustainable material science, and modern urban transportation.

The department provides comprehensive experimental laboratories for surveying, concrete testing, and environmental water quality inspection.

Key Subjects & Core Topics:
Structural Engineering Construction Materials Surveying Transportation Engineering Environmental Engineering Geotechnical Engineering

Practical Learning: Theodolite and Total Station field mapping, soil compaction tests, aggregate sieve analysis, and structural drafting software.

Related Careers: Structural Analyst, Urban Infrastructure Planner, Geotechnical Consultant, Environmental Engineer.
