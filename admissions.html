GENERAL ELIGIBILITY

General Eligibility Information

Higher Secondary (10+2 / Intermediate):

Successful completion of 10+2 examination or equivalent with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry (MPC) from a recognized state or central education board.

Lateral Entry (Direct 2nd Year):

Candidates possessing a recognized Diploma in Engineering or relevant discipline are eligible to seek lateral admission into the second year as per governing admission rules.

State & Entrance Examinations:

Admission criteria, counseling procedures, and merit allocations conform to applicable regulatory guidelines.

Important Regulatory Notice: Actual eligibility criteria, minimum qualifying percentage, entrance exam cut-offs, category reservation rules, and statutory admission quotas depend entirely on applicable state higher education policies and the verified admission guidelines of the institution. This demo site does not assert cutoffs, fees, or admission guarantees.

CHECKLIST

DOCUMENTS CHECKLIST

General list of documents typically required during engineering admissions verification:

  • Academic Marksheets: 10th (SSC) and 12th (Intermediate / 10+2) original scorecards and passing certificates.
  • Identity Proof: Valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar card or equivalent).
  • Photographs: Recent passport-size colored photographs of the candidate.
  • Entrance Examination Rank Card: Applicable state or national engineering entrance exam scorecard/hall ticket.
  • Institutional Certificates: Transfer certificate (TC), conduct certificate, and category certificate where applicable.
