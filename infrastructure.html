CAMPUS TOUR

ACADEMIC & LIVING ENVIRONMENT

Illustrative engineering campus facilities designed to nurture learning, research, and holistic student growth.

FACILITY 01

Academic Buildings & Administrative Block

Spacious multi-storey architectural structures designed with cross-ventilation, wide corridors, departmental staff rooms, and centralized administrative reception desks.

FACILITY 02

Interactive Smart Classrooms

Air-conditioned lecture halls equipped with digital display panels, audiovisual projection systems, ergonomic seating, and high-speed Wi-Fi access for blended engineering instruction.

FACILITY 03

Central Computer Facilities

Dedicated server rooms and connected terminal bays running multi-core processors, Linux and Windows platforms, gigabit fiber network switches, and licensed software suites.

FACILITY 04

Central Knowledge Resource Library

Extensive print collections covering all engineering branches, digital repository terminals for IEEE/e-journal access, quiet reading cubicles, and reference discussion sections.

FACILITY 05

Seminar Hall & Auditorium

Tiered auditorium seating with acoustic paneling, stage illumination, surround sound public address systems, and broadcast recording gear for technical symposiums.

FACILITY 06

Central Engineering Workshops

Dedicated bays for machine fitting, carpentry, tin-smithy, foundry casting, arc welding, and industrial machine tool operations supporting first-year foundation courses.

FACILITY 07

Sports & Recreation Arenas

Outdoor grounds for cricket, football, and track events, alongside floodlit courts for volleyball, basketball, badminton, and an indoor table tennis / chess pavilion.

FACILITY 08

Student Cafeteria & Common Lounges

Hygienic food courts offering balanced meals and refreshments, shaded outdoor courtyard seating, student union club offices, and group study discussion zones.
